# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: lightweight static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, selected because the project is a simple one-page marketing site and the current environment cannot fetch the official scaffold from npm

## Users

Private customers, companies, organizations, and property owners who need installation, diagnostics, repair, or recovery of fiber-optic communication lines.

## Product Purpose

Present the company clearly, explain its services, help visitors understand approximate timing and pricing, and make it easy to contact the company.

## Positioning

An engineering service that explains fiber-optic work in plain language and bases recommendations on the condition of the actual object and measurement results.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from search, recommendations, or direct links and usually need to understand whether the company handles their problem before calling or messaging.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- First release is a single responsive page in Russian.
- Primary action is a phone call or a message.
- Contact links use phone, email, Telegram, and WhatsApp when confirmed.
- Approximate prices may use “от” values or ranges, but final pricing depends on the object.
- The current contact data and company identity are placeholders and must be replaced before publication.
- Do not invent customer logos, reviews, credentials, project counts, or other proof.

## Product Principles

- Be specific about the work.
- Make the next action obvious.
- Prove with real information, never invented authority.
- Explain technical work in accessible language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keep body text readable and high contrast.
- Provide visible keyboard focus states.
- Preserve native details/summary interaction.
- Support reduced motion and narrow mobile screens.
